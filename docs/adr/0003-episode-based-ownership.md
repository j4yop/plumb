# Ownership is decided in episodes, not frame by frame

Deciding the owner per frame shattered one 41-second possession into seven, because a pass
travelling within a metre of a defender flipped the owner for a frame or two and each flip became
another possession for the scorer to punish. Ownership is therefore decided on maximal runs of
control, and episodes shorter than a second are absorbed into their neighbours before they can
become possessions. The metric counts possessions, so the decision has to be made at the level
the metric measures.
